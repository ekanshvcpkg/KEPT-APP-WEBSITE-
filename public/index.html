How it works

Swear it. Prove it. Get paid for keeping it.

01 · SWEAR

Write the Oath.

Pick a goal, the object you'll show, how many days, and what you stake. Solo, or with up to four Keepers.

02 · PROVE

Two photos a day.

Show the object plus a random hand sign before the challenge expires. Checked on-device. The photo never leaves your phone.

03 · SETTLE

Keep it, take the pot.

Every miss costs SKR. When the Oath ends, the people who kept split what the people who missed left behind.

Oaths

Swear it with your people.

Up to four Keepers share one health bar. Every day someone misses, the group loses 20. At zero the Oath breaks and everyone carries the mark. Hold it and your stake comes back with interest. Nobody wins it alone.
